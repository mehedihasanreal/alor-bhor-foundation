About Us

A foundation dedicated to social good.

Alor Bhor Foundation is a Bangladesh-based NGO committed to serving communities through social work, education support, and awareness activities.

Our goal is to stand beside people, encourage positive change, and create opportunities for a brighter tomorrow.

Social Service

Helping people and communities through meaningful support.

Education

Promoting learning, guidance, and educational encouragement.

Awareness

Building awareness on important social and community issues.

Community

Working together with volunteers, supporters, and local people.
