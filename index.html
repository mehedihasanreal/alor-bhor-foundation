Alor Bhor Foundation

Establishment and Goal

Establishment

Driven by compassion and responsibility toward humanity, Alor Bhor Foundation began its journey on February 1, 2018.

Goal

With the vision of building a beautiful and humane society, Alor Bhor Foundation has been working with three core principles: service, education, and awareness.

Service

Standing beside people with care and practical support.

Education

Creating opportunities for learning, growth, and guidance.

Awareness

Encouraging social responsibility and informed action.

Future Plans

Future Plans of Alor Bhor Foundation

  1. Making the highest possible effort toward building a hunger- and poverty-free Bangladesh
  2. Establishing an institute for youth skill development
  3. Establishing a self-development center
  4. Establishing an integrated residential school with multidimensional education opportunities for underprivileged and orphan students

Current Projects

Newly Adopted Plans for Fiscal Year 2025-2026

Alor Bhor Foundation continues its work through planned social service, education, health, and awareness initiatives.

  1. Alor Bhor Mental Health Counseling Program
  2. Alor Bhor Self-Reliance Project
  3. Monthly Skill Development Campaigns
  4. Free Medical Service Project
  5. Mission Marginalized Students: counseling program for children from underprivileged and remote communities
  6. Mission Ananda: providing sports equipment for children and adolescents and raising awareness to reduce device addiction

Completed Projects

Activities Implemented from 2018 to 2025

These completed activities reflect the foundation's ongoing commitment to service, education, emergency response, and awareness.

  1. Alor Bhor Education Mission Project
  2. Winter Gift Project: winter clothing distribution
  3. Providing safe water, food, and rescue services during disaster situations
  4. Providing safe water and hygienic sanitation facilities
  5. Alor Bhor Iftar Event
  6. Alor Bhor Eid Gift Project
  7. Nationwide tree plantation and awareness programs to combat the effects of climate change
  8. Help for Injured
